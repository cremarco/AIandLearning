# Editorial shortcut selection · 7 November 2026

Technical verification identified 71 labels with working learning controls. Editorial review retains **15** complementary examples: **12 videos and 3 live-check summaries**. It removes **56** secondary or overlapping detail slides and **2** repeated demonstrations, removing **58 slides from the shortcut section**.

The decision is about workshop relevance and duplication, not whether the excluded prompts work. The complete technical reviews and original media remain available. Newer video transcripts take precedence over historical differences when judging duplicate workflows.

## Selection criteria

- Support a distinct step in the workshop learning pathway.
- Prefer the clearest demonstrated interaction over cosmetic labels or game themes.
- Retain both student self-directed learning and teacher classroom workflows.
- Describe only controls and behaviour actually observed.
- Compare newer video transcripts before treating historical control differences as distinct capabilities.
