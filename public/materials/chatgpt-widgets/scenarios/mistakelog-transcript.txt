1. Training


MISCONCEPTION

Training simply stores the examples as readable entries inside the model.

Hide correction

Correct explanation

Training updates numerical parameters through optimization to reduce aggregate prediction error.

Reason: The training text supplies a learning signal. Parameters are learned numerical values, not an encyclopedia of readable entries.

2. Inference


MISCONCEPTION

The model must update its weights whenever it generates a response.

Hide correction

Correct explanation

Ordinary inference performs calculations using parameters already available.

Reason: A fixed model can calculate output distributions and select outputs without modifying its learned weights.
