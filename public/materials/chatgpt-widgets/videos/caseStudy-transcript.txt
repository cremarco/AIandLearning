Scenario: Andrea is building a text-generation application. He notices that the model produces different answers after he changes the information supplied to it.

Three situations

1. Training

Andrea trains a model using examples. Prediction errors guide optimization, updating the model's numerical parameters.

2. Inference

Andrea adds “In this chapter, X means Y” to his prompt. The model uses this context while keeping its parameters fixed.

3. Retrieval (RAG)

Andrea retrieves a document. Selected passages become available as context without necessarily changing model weights.
