PROMPT
Explain the difference between training and inference in two short sentences. Answer in English.

RESPONSE
Training is the process of learning patterns from data by adjusting a model’s parameters to reduce prediction errors. Inference is the process of using those learned parameters to generate predictions or outputs for new inputs without changing them.
