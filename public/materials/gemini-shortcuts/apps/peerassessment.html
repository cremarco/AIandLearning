1.1 Parameter Updates vs. Weight Constancy

Distinguishes pretraining parameter optimization from changing prompt text or conversation context.

Expected Evidence from Chapter (Sec. 1.1, 1.2, Tab. 1, Resource B Q1):

Pillar 2 Model Inference & Decoding Architecture
Sections 1.4, 1.5, 1.6 & Figures 3-6

2.1 Flow Architecture & Causal Mask Constraint

Traces the sequence transformation pipeline and explains the autoregressive causal mask rule.

Expected Evidence from Chapter (Sec. 1.4, 1.5, Fig. 3, Fig. 4, Fig. 5, Resource B Q2):
  • Pipeline Flow: Text $\rightarrow$ Token ID $\rightarrow$ Initial Embedding $\rightarrow$ Decoder Blocks (Causal Attention + Feed-Forward + Residuals) $\rightarrow$ Logits $\rightarrow$ Softmax Probabilities $\rightarrow$ Token Selection.
  • Causal Masking Constraint: When predicting the next token, query position $q$ can attend to earlier keys ($k \le q$) and itself, but is strictly blocked from attending to future positions ($k > q$).
  • Query, Key, Value & Attention: Queries compare with keys to weight values. Attention is a mathematical operation; it does not imply human consciousness or fixed head labels like "grammar".
Chapter Ref: Section 1.5 & Figure 5 (Causal Access Mask Matrix)

2.2 Softmax Distribution, Temperature & Probabilities

Evaluates Softmax calculation over full vocabulary, controlled temperature experiments, and truth distinction.

Expected Evidence from Chapter (Sec. 1.6, Fig. 6-7, Tab. 2, Sec. 1.8):
  • Softmax Formula: $p_i = \frac{\exp(z_i / T)}{\sum_j \exp(z_j / T)}$. Calculates probabilities across full vocabulary; top tokens plus remaining mass must equal 100%.
  • Controlled Temperature Experiment: Keeping input and logits fixed, increasing $T$ flattens probability concentration across alternatives without changing logit ranking or model parameters.
  • Probability $\neq$ Truth Error Correction: Corrects the false claim "80% token probability = 80% truth probability", specifying that probability measures continuation likelihood under learned distribution, not truth.
Chapter Ref: Section 1.6 & Section 1.8 ("Correct the error")
