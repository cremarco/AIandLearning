PROMPT BANK — ENGLISH
Original workshop instructions, not validated prompts or a record of live tool tests.

Access agreement
Keep assistive technology and approved accommodations in every activity, including independent checks. A learner may respond orally, in writing or through an equivalent structured representation. Do not infer a diagnosis or lower the intellectual goal.

Planning: “Help me plan how to learn [topic]. First ask what I already know and what I need to be able to do. Offer two possible next steps and explain the trade-off. Do not choose my goal for me.”

Scaffold: “I have tried [attempt]. Do not provide the full solution yet. Ask one question that helps me identify the next step. If I am missing essential background knowledge, explain that point briefly.”

Map critique: “Here are my concepts and labelled relations: [text]. Identify one ambiguous relation and one missing condition. Do not add factual claims unless you mark them as suggestions that need checking.”

Verification: “For each factual claim, distinguish information supported by the supplied source from your inference. Point to the exact passage when available. If the source does not establish the claim, say so.”

Alternative explanation: “Give a plausible counterargument to my position. State which evidence would help distinguish the two explanations. Do not treat agreement with me as the goal.”

Reflection: “Ask me to explain the idea in my own words and apply it to a new case. Give feedback after my attempt, and identify what I should verify independently.”

Accessible version: “Rewrite this explanation in clear, structured language. Preserve the key concepts and uncertainty. Provide headings and an ordered list equivalent to the diagram. Do not infer a disability or reduce the intellectual goal.”

Independent check (access supports retained)
“Pause answer-generating assistance. Ask me to explain the reasoning or apply it to a new example. Keep screen readers, text-to-speech, dictation, translation and approved accommodations available. Accept an oral or written response.”

Verification reminder
Open the original source. Check whether the exact passage supports the claim. Prompts do not guarantee accurate facts, references, translations or accessible output.
