LEARNING PATH WORKBOOK — ENGLISH
Original workshop handout, based on the shared dossier. Not a validated assessment scale.

Access and participation
Choose a written, spoken or structured-map response. Keep screen readers, text-to-speech, dictation, translation and approved accommodations. Independent reasoning means making and explaining your own learning decisions; it does not mean giving up access support.

Scenario
A school is considering an AI assistant for independent study. Recommend a small learning activity and explain what evidence you would collect before expanding it.

Goal: By the end, I will be able to …
Starting point: I already know … / I need to understand …
Success evidence: I will demonstrate this by …
My first attempt: …
Plan: Step 1 … / Step 2 … / Step 3 …
AI support allowed: … / Decisions I keep: …
Sources to check: …
Uncertainty: What is unclear? What evidence is missing? …
Independent content check, retaining access accommodations: Explain / apply / retrieve …
Reflection: What changed, why, and what will I try next? …

1. Make a first attempt using the access supports you need.
2. Compare two verified sources. Mark observation, inference and uncertainty.
3. Ask for one question or objection, if useful; keep the decision yours.
4. Pause answer-generating AI and explain or apply the idea. Retain assistive technology, accessible materials and approved accommodations; oral or written answers are equally welcome.
5. Record one revision and the evidence that caused it.

Debrief
What did the support help you do?
Which decision did you keep?
What would show learning rather than a polished output?
Which barrier remained, and what equivalent route would remove it?
