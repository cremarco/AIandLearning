Your turn: create materials for students

30 minutes · Choose your topic and use any tools you prefer.

Study phase: APPLY · Secondary: CHECK

30-minute practice

  1. Choose a topic and suitable sources

    Choose a topic, your students’ level and reliable sources to work from.

  2. Create materials with your chosen tools

    Make materials for your students: for example a guide, quiz, flashcards, slides or an activity.

  3. Check and prepare to share

    Test the materials, verify facts and answers against the sources, and check student access.
