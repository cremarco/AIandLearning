Study phase: APPLY · Secondary: CHECK

20-minute practice

  1. Choose and install a skill

    Use one of the three reviewed skills, or find a new one. Install it in a compatible host.

  2. Create study material

    Try the skill with your source: make flashcards, a diagram or a study kit.

  3. Test and improve

    Use the material, check it against the source and refine your request.

Your turn: create materials for students

30 minutes · Choose your topic and use any tools you prefer.

Study phase: APPLY · Secondary: CHECK

30-minute practice

  1. Choose a topic and suitable sources

    Choose a topic, your students’ level and reliable sources to work from.

  2. Create materials with your chosen tools

    Make materials for your students: for example a guide, quiz, flashcards, slides or an activity.

  3. Check and prepare to share

    Test the materials, verify facts and answers against the sources, and check student access.

What will you take into your classroom?

  1. One learning goal

    What will your students learn?

    What will help them choose and monitor their next steps?

    Which risk will you address, and how?
