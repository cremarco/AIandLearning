INPUT
Training versus inference in a language model. Explain the difference briefly and give one short example.

ACTUAL RESULT
EXPLANATION
Training a language model means teaching it by showing lots of text so it learns patterns and how words fit together. Inference is when the model uses what it learned to generate or predict text based on new input.

Example: Training is like studying grammar and vocabulary. Inference is like writing a sentence using what you’ve learned.

EXAMPLE FIELD
Training: Teaching the model to understand language by showing it millions of sentences and adjusting its internal settings.
Example: Feeding a model thousands of news articles to help it learn grammar and facts.

Inference: Using the trained model to generate or understand text without changing its settings.
Example: Asking the model to write a summary of today's news after it has already been trained.
